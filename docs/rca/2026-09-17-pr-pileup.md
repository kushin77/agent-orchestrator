## Summary

1. Landing on this fleet requires a green, attributed full `make verify` per
   `governance/landing/engine.py` + AGENTS.md rule 10 (autonomous merge only
   after green verification evidence) — this is by design, not a bug (H4).
2. Master itself is currently red on all four named checks measured
   directly against a clean `origin/master` checkout — `check-lifecycle-reclaim.sh`,
   `check-gate-lock.sh`, `check-fleet-jobs.sh`, and `check-fleet-cron-dev-run.sh`
   — so every lane's verify inherits these reds regardless of the lane's own
   diff (H2 CONFIRMED).
3. Merging stopped cold at 13:47:03Z (PR #1084) while lanes kept opening PRs
   through 14:10Z; per-PR mechanics (mergeable state, commits behind master)
   are NOT the blocker — every sampled open PR is `MERGEABLE`/`CLEAN` and 1–4
   commits behind master (H6 CONFIRMED clean, H3 CONFIRMED cadence mismatch).

## Root cause(s)

**Primary:** Master fails its own gate right now (H2) — `check-lifecycle-reclaim.sh`,
`check-gate-lock.sh`, `check-fleet-jobs.sh`, and `check-fleet-cron-dev-run.sh`
all report real failures (two `ModuleNotFoundError`s in scratch-worktree
construction, one multi-probe fleet-jobs regression, one dev-run baseline
case that fails before any mutant is tried), not timeouts. Since `governance/landing/engine.py`
requires a green, attributed verify before an autonomous merge (AGENTS.md
rule 10), and attribution measures a lane's reds *against* master, a red
master makes every lane's full verify red by inheritance — no lane can
land, however clean its own diff is, until master's own three checks are
fixed.

**Secondary:** The gate-lock permit store (157 leftover 0-byte locks) makes
the box-wide 4-slot cap noisier and harder to reason about than it needs to
be; it is not the current driver (only 3/4 slots held at measurement time)
but is one lever that would tighten under the next PR-opening burst.

## Contributing factors

- Lane *opening* has no shared throughput gate — 13 lanes opened PRs
  independently within a 66-minute window with nothing coordinating against
  landing capacity (H3), so the queue visibly balloons the moment landing
  stalls, even briefly.
- `scripts/check-gate-lock.sh doctor`'s "needing attention" sweep is
  alert-only by design (RCA-0015) and nothing is currently acting on the
  157 leftovers it would report.
- Draft PR #1044 (37 commits behind, conflicting) is dead weight in the
  open-PR count and should not be conflated with the 16 healthy ones when
  someone next triages "why are there so many PRs."

## What not to do (lessons)

- Do not read "16 open PRs" as evidence of per-PR mergeability problems —
  H6 measured them individually and 16/17 are clean; treating this as a
  rebase/conflict problem would fix nothing.
- Do not treat the 157 leftover gate-lock files as the active cause of
  today's stall — the cap was only at 3/4 when measured, and RCA-0015
  already explains why an automated box-wide reaper of these is
  deliberately not implemented; adding one now would reopen that reviewed
  risk (see RCA-0015 above) instead of fixing the actual blocker.
- Do not add a human approval step to "solve" the pile-up — AGENTS.md rule
  10 and `governance/landing/engine.py` are explicit that verification
  replaces the human reviewer; the fix is making the verification true
  (fix master), not slowing landing down further with a review queue.
